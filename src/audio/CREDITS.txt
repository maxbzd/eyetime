## Instrument samples (src/audio/samples)

Short notes of an upright piano, a harp and a cello section, used by the generative focus sound (src/shared/soundscape). Cut to a few seconds,
loudness-normalised and encoded to Ogg Vorbis by scripts-dev/build-samples.mjs.

## Pieces after generative.fm

`src/shared/soundscape/skyline.js` and `gfm.js` are our own implementations of the ideas of four pieces of generative.fm by Alex Bainter
(Skyline, Drones 2, Homage, Apoapsis, Nakaii; code MIT, https://github.com/generative-music/pieces-alex-bainter): instrument samples pitched down
by octaves, re-triggered at random intervals, through long reverbs, slow filters and echoes. The pieces are described there as data; no code or
sample of generative.fm is included (its lo-fi drum samples are not used, the drums are synthesised).
The idea of notes on independent random periods is theirs, and Brian Eno's before.
