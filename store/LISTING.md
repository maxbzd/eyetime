## Name
EyeTime — Focus, Screen Time & Eye Care

## Summary (≤ 132 chars)
Start a focus session in one click, pause distractions gently, see where your time goes and rest your eyes. Free, 100% local.

## Description (EN)
EyeTime helps you do deep work without fighting your browser.

🎯 FOCUS IN ONE CLICK
Start a timed session from the toolbar, the new tab or Alt+Shift+F. Distracting sites pause until it ends — and open again by themselves when it does. Choose Strict mode when you really mean it.

🛡 BLOCKING THAT RESPECTS YOU
Pick categories (social, video, forums, news, games, shopping, messengers) or add your own sites. A short breathing pause before a distracting site beats a lockout. Add work hours and daily limits per site.

📊 HONEST STATISTICS
See time per site, per hour and per day, a calendar heatmap, and plain-language highlights like "your most distracting day was Sunday". Re-classify any site as productive, neutral or distracting with one tap. Export CSV any time.

👁 LOOK AFTER YOUR EYES
20-20-20 reminders based on real, active screen time and a guided 20-second break page.

🏠 A CALM NEW TAB
Clock, focus card, tasks, habits, notes and quick links on a gradient or your own photo. Light and dark themes. Switch it off any time.

📱 SHORT-VIDEO GUARD
After a limit of Shorts / Reels / TikTok / VK Clips scrolling the page goes grayscale and pauses.

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No servers. No analytics. Only domain names and seconds are stored — on your device. The code is open source (MIT).

English and Russian. More languages welcome on GitHub.

## Single purpose (Chrome Web Store field)
Help users focus and manage screen time: track time per site, pause distracting sites during focus sessions, and remind users to rest their eyes.
