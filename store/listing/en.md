## Name
EyeTime — Focus, Screen Time & Eye Care

## Summary (≤ 132 characters)
Focus sessions, gentle pauses before distracting sites, honest screen-time stats and eye care. Free, open source, 100% local.

## Description
EyeTime helps you do deep work without fighting your browser. It is free, open source and keeps everything on your device.

FOCUS IN ONE CLICK
Start a timed session from the toolbar, the new tab, Alt+Shift+F or the command palette (Ctrl/Cmd+K). Pick a mode — Deep work, Sprint, Study — add five more minutes when you are in the flow, and let the break timer do its job. Distracting sites pause until the session ends and open again by themselves. Strict mode is there when you really mean it.

BLOCKING THAT RESPECTS YOU
Choose categories (social, video, forums, news, games, shopping, messengers) or add your own sites. A short breathing pause with your own if–then plan beats a lockout. Add work hours, daily limits per site and a daily "distraction budget".

PLAN THE DAY AND THE WEEK
"Plan my day" fits your priorities into the time you really have. Type "Report tomorrow 14:30 45m !" or "Stretch every weekday" and EyeTime understands — in six languages. Subscribe to your calendar's link (or import a file) to see your next meeting on the focus card. Three goals for the week, recurring tasks, and your tasks in and out as Markdown or CSV.

HONEST STATISTICS
Time per site, hour and day; a day timeline with your focus sessions; focus time by project (#tag); estimate accuracy (planned vs. actual); your best focus hours; "what pulls you" — when a site tempts you, with a one-tap limit; a weekly review and a shareable "My week" picture. Re-classify any site as productive, neutral or distracting with one tap. Export CSV or a Markdown journal.

A CALM NEW TAB
Clock, focus card with a daily goal, tasks with priorities, habits, notes and quick links. Eleven looks, any accent colour, 17 backdrops, real ambient sounds. Light and dark. Switch it off any time.

LOOK AFTER YOUR EYES
20-20-20 reminders based on real, active screen time and a guided 20-second break page.

SHORT-VIDEO GUARD
After a limit of Shorts / Reels / TikTok / VK Clips scrolling, the page goes grayscale and pauses.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No servers. No analytics. Only domain names and seconds are stored — on your device. If you subscribe to a calendar link, EyeTime downloads that one file straight from your browser; the address never leaves your device. Optional settings sync uses your browser's own sync and never includes your history. The code is open source (MIT): https://github.com/maxbzd/eyetime

Languages: English, Russian, Spanish, German, French and Portuguese.
