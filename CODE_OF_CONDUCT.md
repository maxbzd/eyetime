# Code of conduct

EyeTime is a small project for people who want a calmer relationship with their screens. The community around it should feel the same.

**Be kind and specific.** Say what you saw, what you expected and what would help. Assume the other person wants the project to be good.
**Disagree about the work, not the person.** Criticism of code, design, wording or a decision is welcome; contempt, insults, harassment, discrimination and
personal attacks are not, in any language (the project speaks eleven, and not everyone writing in a second one is as fluent as they would like to be: be patient).
**Keep private things private.** Do not post anyone's personal information, and do not paste your own browsing history or tasks into a public issue (*Settings → About →
Report a problem* makes a text that holds none).

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and every other space of the project. The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments and may ask someone to leave a
discussion or the project when these are not kept. To report a problem with someone's conduct, write privately to the maintainer through GitHub
(<https://github.com/maxbzd>); reports are read in confidence.

It is adapted from the ideas of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), which you may read in full.
